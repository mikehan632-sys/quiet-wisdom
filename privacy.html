隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

最后更新 Last updated: 14 September 2026

静悟不收集你的任何个人资料。没有账号，没有分析统计，没有广告，没有第三方追踪。你在应用内做的一切都留在你自己的手机上。

Quiet Wisdom collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your own device.

1. 我们收集什么What we collect

没有。静悟不含任何网络请求代码，不会把资料送到我们或任何第三方的服务器 —— 因为并不存在这样的服务器。

Nothing. Quiet Wisdom contains no networking code and sends no data to us or to any third party — there is no server to send it to.

2. 留在你手机上的资料What is stored on your device

为了让应用记得你的选择，以下资料储存在你手机本机（iOS 的 UserDefaults 与钥匙串）。这些资料不会离开你的设备，我们无法读取。

So the app can remember your choices, the following is stored locally on your device (iOS UserDefaults and Keychain). It never leaves your device and we cannot read it.

安装时间存放于钥匙串，因此删除应用再重装时不会重新计时。它刻意没有开启 iCloud 同步，不会传到你的其他设备。

The install date lives in the Keychain so deleting and reinstalling does not restart the clock. It is deliberately not iCloud-synced and does not travel to your other devices.

3. 购买Purchases

会员由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。付款资料由 Apple 收取与保管，我们永远看不到你的姓名、付款方式或 Apple ID。静悟只向系统询问一个问题：这台设备是否有有效的会员资格。

Membership is handled by Apple's App Store. Payment details are collected and held by Apple and we never see them — not your name, your payment method, or your Apple ID. Quiet Wisdom asks the system one question only: does this device hold a valid entitlement.

Apple 如何处理这些资料，请见 Apple 隐私政策。

For how Apple handles that, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. 赠一句Sending a line to a friend

「赠一句」会生成一条链接，内含句子编号和你写的留言。这条链接由你自己透过你选择的方式传送（讯息、电邮等），静悟不会传送、储存或看到它。

The gift feature builds a link containing a line number and any note you write. You send that link yourself, through whichever app you choose (Messages, Mail, and so on). Quiet Wisdom does not transmit, store, or see it.

5. 没有追踪，没有广告，没有第三方No tracking, no ads, no third parties

  • 没有分析或崩溃报告 SDK — No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • 没有广告与广告识别码（IDFA）— No advertising or IDFA
  • 没有跨应用或跨网站追踪 — No tracking across apps or websites
  • 完全没有第三方程式库 — No third-party libraries at all
  • 不出售、不分享、不转移任何资料 — Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred

6. 儿童Children

静悟不收集任何人的资料，包括儿童。应用内没有社交功能、没有使用者生成的公开内容，也没有外部广告。

Quiet Wisdom collects no data from anyone, children included. There are no social features, no publicly shared user content, and no external advertising.

7. 你的权利Your rights

由于我们不持有你的任何资料，没有可供索取、更正或删除的资料。删除应用即会移除本机储存的一切；钥匙串中的安装时间可透过重设设备移除。

Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to provide, correct, or delete. Deleting the app removes everything stored locally; the Keychain install date is removed by resetting the device.

8. 政策变更Changes to this policy

若静悟未来加入需要处理资料的功能，本页会在该功能发布之前更新，并标注新的更新日期。

If Quiet Wisdom ever gains a feature that requires handling data, this page will be updated before that feature ships, with a new date above.
